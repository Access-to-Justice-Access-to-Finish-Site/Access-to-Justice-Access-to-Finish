Our Purpose

Document. Question. Advocate. Rebuild.

This is no longer a campaign for readmission. It is a public-interest project grounded in careful storytelling, documentary accountability, and advocacy.

Current focus: to document my experience accurately, preserve a complex record, encourage public accountability, support better institutional practices, and make room for a future beyond Western Law.
01

Preserve the record

Organize the chronology, decisions, correspondence, and recorded material so the history can be understood without stripping away context.

02

Publish responsibly

Verify sources, distinguish allegations from findings, and withhold private, privileged, medical, or irrelevant third-party information.

03

Invite accountability

Ask what institutions owe people who report harm, seek accommodation, or become trapped between overlapping systems.

04

Advocate for change

Use the documented experience to support more accessible, timely, and humane responses in education and professional systems.

05

Build what comes next

Create room for professional, creative, and advocacy work that is not dependent on readmission or institutional permission.

What I am not asking the public to do

I am not asking anyone to harass, threaten, or target an individual or institution. I am not asking readers to treat a submission as a ruling or a personal account as an adjudicated finding. The goal is understanding, accountability, and constructive change.
