What I Am Asking For

A fair, safe and timely pathway to complete my JD.

The request is practical and present-focused: an authorized academic determination, appropriate accommodations and a workable plan to finish the degree.

Locked public ask: I am asking Western University to provide a fair, safe and timely pathway for me to complete my JD, including an authorized academic determination, appropriate accommodations, a safe return structure, and the ability to complete my remaining degree requirements.
01

Authoritative academic status

A prompt, written determination from the person or office with authority to decide my present academic status and eligibility to return.

02

A timely 2026–27 process

A process that recognizes the academic calendar and does not leave the decisive question unresolved while another term passes.

03

Accommodations and boundaries

Appropriate academic accommodations, predictable communication channels and clear boundaries that support a safe, stable return focused on completing coursework.

04

A completion plan

A practical academic plan identifying the remaining degree requirements and how they can be completed.

05

An end to circular referrals

A present-tense answer rather than indefinite cycling between internal, external, legal and administrative routes that do not themselves determine academic eligibility.

What I am not asking the public to do

I am not asking members of the public to decide every allegation, interpret every legal filing, or assume bad faith by every person involved. The record is available so people can understand the chronology and evaluate source material in context.

The immediate objective is narrower: a fair and usable academic outcome before another opportunity to finish is lost.
