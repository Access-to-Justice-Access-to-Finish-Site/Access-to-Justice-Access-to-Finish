Release 01 · first full recordingFinal review before upload

Western Human Rights Office meeting

Fall 2024 · Participants included university representatives and students

This meeting records concerns raised with the university, including accounts of racial discrimination, academic treatment, requests for accommodation, trauma-related impacts, and the consequences described for education and career planning. It also records how those concerns were discussed and what guidance was offered.

To protect participants, the edited version omits selected third-party contributions. The original is preserved separately. Descriptions of others' experiences are attributed to the people reporting them; they are not presented as independent findings.

How this archive is being prepared

Recordings are edited for relevant privacy concerns, with the source material retained. Publication notes will identify meaningful omissions and distinguish what is directly heard from interpretation. Short contextual excerpts may also appear on individual year pages; they are separate from these planned longer releases.

This is an ongoing record. New material will be posted in phases as it is reviewed.
