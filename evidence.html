How to read this evidence

This page is not a document dump. Each anchor record is tied to a specific proposition: the formal withdrawal decision, the return route later identified, the preserved professional opportunity, the new 2026 institutional status activity, the closing of an external completion route, and the legal step taken as the Fall term approached.

The records are not presented as self-explanatory proof of every allegation in the broader dispute. Where a document is not a final academic decision, the page says so. Where automated records do not themselves establish reinstatement, the page says so. Where a filing shows only that a legal step was taken, the page says so.

What is intentionally not public here

The full HRTO evidentiary record, medical documentation, private addresses and telephone numbers, unrelated student or third-party material, privileged legal advice, mediation material, settlement communications, and without-prejudice correspondence are outside this public evidence set.
