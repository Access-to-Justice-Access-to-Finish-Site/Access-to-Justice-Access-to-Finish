How to read the evidence

A large record can create the illusion of clarity while actually making the central question harder to see. This evidence hub therefore uses an issue-first structure: what decision was needed, what route was used, what answer was given, and what remained unresolved.

Documents will not be presented as self-explanatory proof of broad conclusions. Context matters. Where a record is disputed, incomplete or capable of more than one interpretation, the public note should say so.

Privacy and redaction

Personal medical information, unrelated third-party details, private contact information and material that should not be publicly distributed will remain redacted or withheld. The purpose of transparency is verification, not exposure.
