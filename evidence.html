Find the source in context.

This index distinguishes firsthand accounts, reported experiences, university statements, procedural records and formal decisions. Inclusion does not mean every disputed allegation has been adjudicated.

Public redaction standard: This archive presents dated documents and source descriptions, not unredacted tribunal files. Personal addresses and contact details, student identifiers, medical and sexual-violence disclosures, private messages, third-party identities, professional references, privileged or confidential communications, and unrelated photographs are excluded. A source listed here is not necessarily a publicly released document.

How to read this index

Dating: Entries follow the date of the event or original source, not the year of later litigation. Year-only entries are used where the supporting material does not establish a specific day. Original document references are retained in the private research file.

Firsthand account describes what I say I experienced. Reported account describes what another person communicated. University statement records the institution's own wording. Formal decision records an administrative outcome. Filing records what a party alleged or requested, not a finding.
