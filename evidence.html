This index distinguishes firsthand accounts, reported experiences, university statements, procedural records and formal decisions. Inclusion does not mean every disputed allegation has been adjudicated.

Public redaction standard: This archive publishes source descriptions and exhibit identifiers, not the unredacted tribunal volumes. Personal addresses and contact details, student identifiers, medical and sexual-violence disclosures, private messages, third-party identities, professional references, privileged or confidential communications, and unrelated photographs are excluded. A source listed here is not necessarily a publicly released document.

How to read this index

Dating: Entries follow the date of the event or original source, not the year of later litigation. A range or “date to verify” means the underlying record has not yet established a more precise date. Exhibit codes are preserved to support source matching without exposing private records.

Firsthand account describes what I say I experienced. Reported account describes what another person communicated. University statement records the institution's own wording. Formal decision records an administrative outcome. Filing records what a party alleged or requested, not a finding.
