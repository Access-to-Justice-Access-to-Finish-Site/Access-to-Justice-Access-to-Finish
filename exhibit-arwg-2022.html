The report in its original context

This October 2022 Western Law report documents the working group's findings about racism at the law school and sets out 53 recommendations. Pages 18–20 discuss reported experiences and survey findings; Appendix A lists the recommendations. The report is a primary institutional source, not a determination of any individual complaint.

Source: Western Law, Anti-Racism Working Group Final Report (October 2022). Also indexed in the supporting record as Volume IV, A-070–A-119.
