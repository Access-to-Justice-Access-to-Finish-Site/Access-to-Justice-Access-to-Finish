The campaign, in plain language.

Short answers to the questions most likely to arise before reading the full story, chronology and evidence record.

Start here: The immediate objective is a fair, safe and timely pathway to complete the JD. The wider record provides context; it does not change the narrow present-focused ask.
What is Access to Justice: Access to Finish asking for?

A prompt, authorized academic determination about present status and return eligibility, appropriate accommodations and communication boundaries, and a practical plan for completing the remaining degree requirements.

Read the full public ask →

Is the public being asked to decide the legal or human-rights claims?

No. The campaign does not ask readers to adjudicate every disputed fact, interpret every filing or assume bad faith by every person involved. The immediate question is whether there can be a fair and workable academic path forward now.

Why make the record public now?

Academic time is perishable. The Fall 2026 term begins September 9, 2026. The campaign makes a complicated record understandable while keeping attention on the present decision needed to preserve a realistic path to degree completion.

What evidence will be published?

The public evidence hub is designed around redacted anchor records, a verified chronology and contextual notes explaining what each item establishes and what it does not. Records are added only after privacy and redaction review.

See the evidence standard →

Why are some records withheld or redacted?

Transparency does not require publishing private medical information, unrelated third-party details, personal contact information or other material that should not be publicly distributed. The purpose of publication is verification, not exposure.

What does a “workable path to finish” mean?

It means a written answer from the appropriate academic authority, a clear plan identifying remaining requirements, and a return structure that is stable enough for the academic work to be completed without indefinite circular referrals between separate processes.

How can supporters help constructively?

Useful support includes responsible sharing, introductions to journalists or relevant professionals, informed professional support, and keeping attention on verified records and the practical completion objective. Harassment, threats, pile-ons and targeted abuse are not requested.

Is this campaign separate from art or business work?

Yes. Campaign, media and support inquiries are being kept separate from art and business communications. A dedicated campaign contact channel will be added before public launch.

Where should journalists start?

Start with the press page for the immediate framing, then review the timeline and evidence standards. The long-form story explains how the different academic, administrative, human-rights and legal routes became part of the wider context.
