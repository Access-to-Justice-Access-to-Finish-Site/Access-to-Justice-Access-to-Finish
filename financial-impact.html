Tuition and student debt

The record documents $10,200 in repayable OSAP borrowing for September 2024–April 2025. The same funding summary records $7,052 in grants and $17,252 in total assistance. This is one academic year’s documented borrowing—not a claim that $10,200 is the entire present loan balance.

That distinction matters. Grants, loans, tuition charges and the current NSLSC balance are different figures. The page will show a full lifetime student-debt total only when a current loan statement verifies it.
