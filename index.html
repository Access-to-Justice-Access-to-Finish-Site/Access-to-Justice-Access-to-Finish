What happens when someone seeks help, follows the processes available to them, and still finds their future caught between institutions?

This is a personal account of barriers to education, the pursuit of accountability, and the decision to build a future beyond them.

01 / What happened to me

More than a degree.

My experience at Western University's Faculty of Law changed the course of my education and professional plans. I have raised concerns about discrimination, harassment, institutional responses and the barriers I encountered while seeking a resolution.

I am sharing my account alongside a carefully organized record so readers can distinguish my experience, the documents, and the questions that remain contested.

I am no longer asking to return to Western Law to finish my degree. This project is about telling my story, pursuing accountability, and reclaiming the freedom to move forward.
