Read the storyView the timelineReview the evidence

I spent years trying internal, administrative, human-rights and legal routes. I kept the records. Another academic year is about to begin, and I still need a current, authorized decision about how I can return and finish.

This campaign makes the record understandable and gives people concrete ways to help.

Why now

The Fall 2026 term begins September 9, 2026. The immediate issue is not only what happened before. It is whether there will be a fair, authorized and workable academic path forward now.

What this campaign is asking for

The public does not need to decide every legal or human-rights issue. The immediate request is narrower: a present academic resolution that allows the degree to be completed safely and fairly.

01

An authorized decision

A clear, written determination of present academic status and return eligibility.

02

A workable return

Appropriate accommodations, communication boundaries and a safe structure for completing the academic year.

03

A path to completion

A practical academic plan for completing the remaining JD requirements without being cycled indefinitely between processes.

When access to justice becomes a maze

This is not a simple student-versus-university story. It is about what happens when multiple systems exist on paper, but the person using them is repeatedly redirected while the practical problem remains unresolved.

“The question is not whether there were processes. The question is whether those processes produced a usable path forward.”
