What happened after I contacted the Human Rights Office

Fall 2024: I reported concerns about discrimination, accommodations and academic treatment. The meeting was recorded.

March 31, 2025: Subsequent correspondence addressed the timing and handling of my complaint.

April–May 2025: A possible academic completion plan was discussed but not approved. An academic-status decision followed on May 2.

June 10, 2025: I submitted concerns to Ontario's Ombudsman.

Before Western Law

A life already in motion.

Before September 2021, I was living independently, developing an acrylic-painting career, accepting commissions, volunteering, and building Project Aaron in memory of my brother. The initiative used memorial portraits to support families affected by homicide and received public recognition, including a March 31, 2021 Toronto Star feature.

I entered Western Law with purpose, optimism, student-leadership ambitions and an existing commitment to public service. The pre-law chapter and the hopeful first semester remain distinct from what followed.
