Media framing

This is not best understood as a conventional student-versus-university story. The public-interest question is what happens when a student uses multiple systems intended to provide oversight or remedy, yet the practical question of how to finish the degree remains unresolved as academic deadlines continue to pass.

Interview principles

The campaign is committed to documentary accuracy, clear separation between fact and interpretation, respect for privacy, and language that does not require vilifying every respondent or institutional actor.
