The separate academic Continuing Enrolment process led Sherrin to require an explanation for continued enrolment by 11 April. What happened: the Human Rights Office had been reviewing allegations involving Sherrin before his withdrawal letter; the student notified Raja of that letter; HR later held the internal complaint in abeyance while the academic process continued. The student alleged reprisal. The evidence establishes timing, notice and institutional responses; it does not independently establish retaliatory motive. Detailed policy connections ↗

What was requested, what worked and what remained costly

Requested: temporary Zoom participation when commuting from Milton, safety concerns or health circumstances made campus attendance difficult; neutral, independent handling of disputed academic decisions; safe channels for communication and a viable path to complete the JD. Documented support: on 10 September 2024 the EDI office coordinated a compassionate Zoom link, which the course professor provided, with Academic Administration copied. In 2025, GBSV and wellness supports and the EDI office also offered practical assistance. Unresolved: the scope of remote access, expense of repeated travel, reported campus and library harassment, and having to navigate academic processes involving professors named in complaints.

Comparable arrangements, without identifying other students: an anonymized student account describes use of Zoom and scheduling adjustments for safety reasons in another GBV matter. A separate student in the WHRO record described concerns about how a classmate’s accent was treated. These accounts contribute context; they are not findings about the people involved or proof the situations were identical. The WHRO discussion also addressed the burden of repeatedly reporting upsetting events.

Financial effect: the record identifies delayed student funding, loss of family financial support, inability to afford travel, time spent on repeat meetings and documentation, and later application/transcript expenses. Totals will be stated only when supported by payment records. Read the linked evidence and August 2024 remote-access discussion ↗.
