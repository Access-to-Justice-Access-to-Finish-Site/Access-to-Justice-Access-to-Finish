Timeline

A chronology of process, decisions, and change.

This working chronology preserves key developments while distinguishing verified dates, source descriptions, and disputed interpretations.

Dates and descriptions must be checked against primary sources before public launch. Historical requests to return are included for accuracy; they are not the project’s current objective.
February 2023

Early faculty meeting

A recorded meeting forms part of the documented history. Audio and transcript remain unpublished pending source and privacy review.

August 16, 2024

Recorded administrative meeting

The working index identifies discussion of withdrawal/readmission, communication expectations, remote options, and check-ins.

August 21, 2024

Petition decision

The working packet describes a decision converting specified grades to withdrawals and addressing withdrawal and conditions. Exact language remains subject to redaction review.

March 7, 2025

Withdrawal letter

Volume IV, Tab O identifies a letter concerning presumptive withdrawal from the JD program. Disputed aspects are not presented as adjudicated findings.

March 31 & May 2, 2025

Further procedural decisions

The packet indexes a human-rights office response concerning timeliness and abeyance, followed by a continuing-enrolment petition decision.

June–August 2026

Return-path requests and status signals

Requests, account activity, and institutional communications raised questions about academic status, good standing, and available routes.

August 21, 2026

Judicial review filed

A filing challenged the May 2025 decision and the 2026 administration of academic status and return eligibility. Its existence is not proof that the claims will succeed.

October 2026

A new advocacy direction

The project shifts away from requesting a return to Western Law and toward public storytelling, accountability, advocacy, and rebuilding.
