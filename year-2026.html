Support and practical costs in 2026

Professional support that I value: The articling employer approved an additional deferral and confirmed that the position would remain available for the 2027–28 period, provided I met the normal eligibility conditions. I am grateful that the opportunity was preserved while I was seeking a path to complete my JD. No employer, office location or staff member is identified in this public account.

Cost of trying available academic routes: Applying for transfer or visiting-student options required researching differing rules and deadlines, ordering transcripts, paying application fees where applicable, seeking a Letter of Permission and following up with university departments. Travel from Milton to London for meetings and attempts to resolve academic issues created additional costs and time burdens. The financial impact includes both out-of-pocket costs and the disruption to professional plans; figures remain unquantified until matched against source records.

What the evidence illustrates: Some institutions and individuals responded constructively, including the articling employer and staff who explained requirements. The unresolved questions concerned Western's authority to permit continued study or an external completion route and the accommodations needed to pursue it. This chronology is about institutional responsibility and viable remedies, not criticizing the people who offered assistance.

February 25, 2026
Documented correspondence and outcome

Osgoode explains why an upper-year transfer was not available

After I inquired about transferring my remaining JD credits, Osgoode Hall Law School advised that its transfer category was for first-year students. As an upper-year student, I could instead apply as a visiting student on a Letter of Permission and graduate from Western.

This distinction mattered: the available Osgoode route still required Western’s authorization.

Evidence: Osgoode admissions response dated February 25, 2026.

June 7–July 6, 2026
Documented correspondence and outcome

Western confirms that I could not obtain an LOP in my academic status

On June 7 I followed up with the dean’s office and Student Services, explained that I had begun receiving-school applications, and requested the Letter of Permission and correction of my academic record.

On July 6, Student Services replied that I was not in good standing and that eligibility to return to Western required an SRBA appeal. He again directed me to the University Ombudsperson about whether an appeal remained possible. Thus, the authorization required by receiving schools had not been issued.

Evidence: June 7, 2026 request and Student Services’ July 6, 2026 response.
